Company Details

Who We Are

WELD-BRAU is a premier boiler making firm specialized in Ground Engaging Tools (G.E.T.) and heavy machinery welding. We are built on technical expertise and a deep understanding of mining and construction machinery.

Our Mission

To empower the earthmoving sector by providing wear solutions that significantly reduce operational costs and equipment downtime through superior craftsmanship.

Our Values

Durability, Precision, and Integrity. We don't just weld; we engineer strength. Every project is executed with a focus on long-term reliability.

How We Work

We import the toughest wear parts in the world for all makes and models of machinery including Crushers, Excavators, Bulldozers, Wheel Loaders, Dump Trucks and other earth moving machinery.
We are trusted advisors and preferred suppliers in Lesotho to the largest mines, quarries, and civil construction firms. We have the parts, services and expertise to keep your machinery moving.

Where We Are Found

Our main workshop is located at Q-Center, Ha-Abia in Maseru, Lesotho. We can be reached on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, & E-mail. Links available below.

Our Services

  • 01. Manufacture of O.E.M. & replacement attachments for Excavators, TLBs, Dozers, and more.
  • 02. Specialized suppliers of Ground Engaging Tools (G.E.T.).
  • 03. Heavy machinery & equipment structural welding.
  • 04. Chromium & Tungsten Carbide embedding (Hardfacing).
  • 05. Precision line boring services.
